Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LT PROJECTS handles information received through this website.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Introduction

LT PROJECTS respects your privacy and handles website information for the purposes described below. This policy applies to visitors to this website and information submitted through its enquiry forms.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, the website asks for your full name, phone number, email address, property interest, and message. Please provide only information you are comfortable sharing for your enquiry.

Information Submitted Through Enquiry Forms

Enquiry form details are placed into a WhatsApp message addressed to LT PROJECTS so the team can respond to your property enquiry. The form does not create an account or take payment.

Automatically Collected Information

Google Analytics is installed on the website and may collect analytics information such as page views, device or browser information, and general usage events according to Google's policies and your browser or consent settings. The website does not use a server-side account system.

How We Use Information

LT PROJECTS may use submitted enquiry details to understand your request, arrange a response, discuss site visits or property requirements, and communicate about that enquiry.

Property Enquiries and Communications

By submitting an enquiry or contacting LT PROJECTS, you consent to being contacted about your request through the contact details you provide. You can ask us to stop enquiry-related communications at any time.

Cookies

The website may use technologies associated with Google Analytics, including cookies or similar identifiers, to understand website usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Analytics and Advertising Services

Google Analytics is present in the website code. No Meta Pixel, Google Ads tag, or reCAPTCHA integration was found in the website code.

Third-Party Services

The website links to or loads services including Google Analytics, Google Fonts, Ionicons, Google Maps, WhatsApp, and the listed social media platforms. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

Data Security

LT PROJECTS takes reasonable practical steps to handle enquiry information responsibly. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Data Retention

Enquiry information may be retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the enquiry, maintain relevant business records, or meet applicable requirements. The website itself does not state a fixed retention period.

User Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you about an enquiry. Contact us using the details below. You can also manage cookies and analytics preferences through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

External Links

The website contains links to external websites and services. LT PROJECTS is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites.

Changes to Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
